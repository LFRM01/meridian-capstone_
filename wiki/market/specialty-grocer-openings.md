# Where Specialty Grocers Are Opening

Answer to a wiki query: what do our sources say about where specialty grocers are opening, filed back as a page per this wiki's Query workflow.

## Inside Meridian's footprint (LA / Orange / Ventura)

- Trader Joe's is opening a new store in Anaheim Hills, Orange County,
  per its 2026 store-growth plan. [Trader Joe's unveils plans for 8 more stores - Grocery Dive](https://www.grocerydive.com/news/trader-joes-store-growth-2026/816131/)
- Sprouts is adding in-store cafes (not new standalone stores) at
  Huntington Beach, Glendale, La Verne, Diamond Bar, and West Covina —
  and, just outside the footprint in San Bernardino County, Rancho
  Cucamonga. [Sprouts to open 20 in-store cafes across Southern California - Grocery Dive](https://www.grocerydive.com/news/sprouts-farmers-market-klatch-coffee-shop-instore-restaurant-food-service-specialty-grocer/761166/)
- Gelson's is piloting two small-format "ReCharge" micromarkets (5,400
  and 3,000 sq ft) — our source doesn't give their location(s). [New grocery formats, store growth and investment signal sector in motion - ICSC](https://www.icsc.com/news-and-views/icsc-exchange/new-grocery-formats-store-growth-and-investment-signal-sector-in-motion)

## Nationally (no location detail beyond "nationwide")

- Sprouts added 35 stores / 840K sq ft in 2024; Trader Joe's added 34
  stores / 450K sq ft in 2024. [Grocery Report 2025: Key Market Insights - JLL](https://www.jll.com/en-us/insights/market-perspectives/grocery-tracker)
- Aldi (a value grocer, not specialty) opened 180 stores in 2025 with
  another 180 planned for 2026. [Grocery Winners Are Built For Value, Fresh Format And Convenience - JLL](https://www.jll.com/en-ca/insights/market-perspectives/grocery-winners-are-built-for-value-fresh-format-and-convenience)

## My own conclusion (not stated by any source)

The only two location-specific data points we have — Trader Joe's
Anaheim Hills store and Sprouts' SoCal cafe cities — both fall inside or
right at the edge of Meridian's own three-county footprint. That's too
thin a sample (two chains, a handful of cities) to call a trend, but
it's a reasonable prompt to ask Dana whether she's noticed
specialty-grocer expansion specifically near Meridian's own stores.
