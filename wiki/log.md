## 2026-09-19 — Ingest: JLL Grocery Tracker report

- Source: [Grocery Report 2025: Key Market Insights](https://www.jll.com/en-us/insights/market-perspectives/grocery-tracker)
  (JLL)
- Pages touched: `market/market-research.md` (industry trends section +
  Open questions)
- Key takeaway: grocery-anchored retail space is genuinely scarce
  nationally (3.5% vacancy, minimal new supply, ~19M sq ft less

  faster than any other retail subtype — JLL's own analysis says this
  will constrain expanding grocers' ability to find space. Extended the
  existing open question about investor competition to also cover
  physical space scarcity, since both bear on whether Meridian's
  lease-takeover growth model still works as cheaply as it has.
