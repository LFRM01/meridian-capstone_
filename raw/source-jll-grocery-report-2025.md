## Grocery-Anchored Real Estate Fundamentals

**Vacancy & Supply:**
Grocery-anchored retail vacancy compressed to 3.5% in Q4 2024, down 10 basis points year-over-year and 100 basis points below the pandemic peak of 4.5% in mid-2021. The sector experienced "minimal new supply additions" with less than 100,000 square feet in net deliveries for the second consecutive year—dramatically below the 2015-2019 average of 1.1 million square feet annually.

**Rent Growth:**
Grocery-anchored retail achieved the highest annual rent growth across all retail subtypes, rising 3.1% year-over-year in Q4 2024, outpacing neighborhood and power centers (both 2.9%). This reflects intense competition for limited space among expanding grocers.

**Investment Activity:**
Despite challenging capital markets, 2024 investment in grocery-anchored retail surpassed 2023 levels, reaching $7.0 billion (up 1.4%). The second half saw particularly strong activity with transaction volume rising 34%. Average price per square foot reached a record $209 in 2024. Investment diversification increased, with private capital's share declining from 74% (2023) to 68% (2024), while REITs and grocery operators significantly expanded participation.

## Grocery Industry Growth & Format Trends

**Fastest Expanders:**

- **Aldi:** Opened 105 locations in 2024, adding 2.3 million square feet, maintaining momentum from record 2023 expansion. Plans to open 225+ stores in 2025 and 800 by 2028.

- **Publix:** Added 43 locations and 2+ million square feet, with over 50% opening in Florida; now operates across eight Southeastern states.

- **Specialty Grocers Surge:** Sprouts opened 35 stores (840,000 sq ft); Trader Joe's added 34 locations (450,000 sq ft); Fresh Market expanded with 8 locations (200,000 sq ft).

- **Discount Grocer Growth:** Grocery Outlet expanded through the United Grocery Outlet acquisition (40-store chain) to strengthen Southeast presence.

**Foot Traffic Patterns:**
Industry-wide grocery visits reached 17.2 billion in 2024 (1.0% increase from 2023). Discount and specialty grocers captured disproportionate growth: Aldi's foot traffic rose 51.2% since 2019 to over 900 million visits; Grocery Outlet increased 48.7% to 130 million visits; Trader Joe's grew 24.7% from 2019 to 390 million visits in 2024.

## West Coast & Regional Activity

**Save Mart (West Coast):**
Acquired by Canadian conglomerate Jim Pattison Group in June 2024 (over 190 stores). Subsequently launched "In-Store Connect" retail media network through Quad/Graphics partnership in April 2024.

**Regional Expansion Patterns:**
H-E-B (Southwest) added 9 Texas locations (1+ million sq ft). The report notes that discount grocers are "actively expanding in the South" and specialty grocers are "strategically strengthening limited presence in certain markets while sprouting in new markets."

## Private Label & Consumer Behavior

Private-label brands captured 20.7% market share in 2024 (up from 19.1% in 2021), with sales exceeding $270 billion—up 23.6% over four years. Grocers expanded offerings: Kroger's "Our Brands" generated $31+ billion; Albertsons' comparable line produced $16.5+ billion. Grocery Outlet launched "GO Brands" (discount segment); Sprouts introduced "Real Root" personal care line; Amazon debuted "Amazon Saver" with items under $5.

## Vacancy & Availability Pressure

Available grocery-anchored retail space decreased 7.5 million square feet from pandemic highs and nearly 19 million square feet—"nearly half"—since post-recession peaks. Net absorption totaled 300,000 square feet in 2024 (down 37.9% from 2023), indicating supply constraints will "impact the long-term growth plans of expanding grocers as they struggle to find desirable space in ideal locations."

## E-Commerce & SNAP Integration

Grocery e-commerce reached nearly $204 billion in 2024 (10.7% increase), projected to surpass $270 billion by 2028 with a 7.4% compound annual growth rate. Over 41 million Americans participate in SNAP (12% of population). Third-party platforms—including Instacart, GoPuff, Uber Eats, Shipt, and Thrive Market—now accept SNAP EBT benefits, broadening accessibility.

## Mergers & Acquisitions

Notable 2024 activity included: SpartanNash's acquisition of Fresh Encounter (49 supermarkets across Kentucky, Indiana, Ohio); Hy-Vee's acquisition of Strack & Van Til (22-store expansion into Indiana); Aldi's Southeastern Grocers acquisition (conversion of 220 Winn-Dixie/Harvey Supermarkets locations).
